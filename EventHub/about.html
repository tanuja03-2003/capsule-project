About EventHub

Event ticketing and venue access platform — Capsule Project.

Our idea

EventHub brings event discovery, ticket booking and venue entry into one simple platform. The goal is to make the customer journey clear while keeping the application architecture suitable for a medium-sized academic project.

Core modules

  • Event discovery and event management
  • Seat selection and ticket booking
  • Digital ticket generation
  • Venue access and ticket validation
